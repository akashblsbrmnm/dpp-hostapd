TEST 4.1 — MAC with colons (invalid per DPP spec)
---------------------------------------------------------------
ba-cli Device.WiFi.DataElements.Network.ProvisionedDPP.1.DPPURI="'DPP:C:81/6;M:aa:bb:cc:dd:ee:ff;K:MDkwEwYHKoZIzj0CAQYIKoZIzj0DAQcDIgADURzxmttZoIRIPWGoQMV00XHWCAQIhXruVWOz0NjlkIA=;;'"

  ERROR: Invalid MAC in M field: 'aa:bb:cc:dd:ee:ff' — must be 12 hex digits
